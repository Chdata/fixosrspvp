One big premise of [[FixOSRSPvP]] is that [[The Polling System is Incompatible with PvP]].

We would really like to bring into question whether or not the polling system can even really possibly "work" with PvP and whether or not supermajority polling works in a climate where [[The community is divided.]]

1. Can the health and integrity of PvP be considered equally as important to the health and integrity of the overall game and its economy?
2. Is the polling system really serving us for PvP in its current form?
3. We're not saying everything has to be unpolled, but can we take a critical look at what could be unpolled?
4. Can we poll PvP changes in different ways like with [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]]?
5. Would it be possible for Jagex to do a more iterative process with less polling and more of "let's just try [[Live-Beta-Testing]] some stuff and see how we feel about it?"

# What is Jagex already doing unpolled?

1. Fixes for overpowered or abusive metas: Freeze logging, Dinh's Bulwark tanking, teams abusing singles mechanics, and dropping items or food to dodge losses or feed friends, the Crimson Kisten nearly one-shotting. Most of these are "balance changes" and should just be done as integrity changes.
   
   We've had multiple situations already where we tried to poll things like this multiple times (item drops in the Wilderness for example), where ultimately they had to come through as an integrity change.
   
   [[FixOSRSPvP]] is saying we need to recognize this pattern and move to its inevitable outcome faster.

2. Post-release tuning of new systems is already done unpolled. Jagex already does this for new updates like Sailing and Raids. We need more of this. Yet when it comes to Soul Wars, Castle Wars, and the PvP Arena, Jagex just gave up for years instead of trying more stuff to fix the problems.

# What needs to be unpolled in PvP?

1. Balance Changes / Zero-Sum Changes, unless it's a balance change being introduced by a Reward being added to the game. Generally, balance changes cannot realistically be polled to both sides.
2. Negative-Sum Changes (e.g., [[Toxic Blowpipe Nerf]])
3. [[Integrity & Cheating]] Fixes - Deterring Bots, adding systems to deter boosting, AFKing, and rigging minigames.
4. If a change was made unpolled, it should be allowed to be reverted unpolled.
5. Changes that strictly only affect PvP should be looked at on a case-by-case basis to be considered unpolled. For example, changing how Bounty Hunter works should be unpolled. But something like the Wilderness is questionable.

# What needs to be "semi-polled" in PvP?

1. "Should we rework this existing PvP content?" -> Instead of polling it from the start, I recommend just trying it with [[Live-Beta-Testing]] then later deciding if we actually want to keep it. There are many rework ideas in our [[Suggestions Directory]], so I recommend making use of [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]] to decide what gets developed first, or I recommend only looking at one idea at a time.
   
   Supporting [[FixOSRSPvP]] means greenlighting Jagex to just try stuff with PvP.

# What should stay polled in PvP?

1. Content Changes / Positive-Sum Changes / "Should this new content be added to the game?"
2. Rewards, including new armor sets and weapons. Even though these often count as "Balance Changes", I do think suddenly releasing unpolled rewards would be going too far.

**Balance Change Polling Double-Bind:**
- Balance Changes need to be unpolled.
- New Armour or Weapon Rewards are Balance Changes.
- Rewards need to be polled.

# Balance Changes, Zero-Sum, and Polling

We know from experience that nerfs almost unanimously fail in polls and later have to come through as unpolled integrity changes.
- Toxic Blowpipe Nerf
- Changes to how items dropped in the Wilderness work.
- Dragon Spear Stun Nerf
- Various other things.

Players will never vote "yes" to nerfing themselves.

This presents a problem in PvP because any balance change at all means buffing one side, and nerfing the other.

This meaning almost all balance changes will automatically fail a poll unless they favor PvMers, who make up 90% of the game versus 10% PKers. See: [[OSRS Population Stats]]
