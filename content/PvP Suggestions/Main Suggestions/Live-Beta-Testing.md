As part of [[FixOSRSPvP]], I propose we do more "Live-Beta-Testing."

There's a few different "styles" of beta-testing we could try.

# Unlimited Beta-Testing

This is the usual way we do beta-testing.

Players are given max stats and a chest that provides most items in the game.

# Limited Import Beta-Testing

This would be like our usual beta-testing worlds, but you get to import your character's stats and bank only once per week.

The world does not provide free stats or items.

This style of beta-testing might be necessary for Wilderness concepts because we can't really have a sense of "risk" if we are given free items.

Additionally, it's harder for players to judge how a change will affect their actual account if they are just given max stats.

We could do a sort of hybrid-testing where we do 2 weeks of **Limited Import Beta-Testing** before 2 weeks of **Unlimited Beta-Testing**.

#  Live-Beta-Testing

This would entail making a temporary change to the live game on every world just to try it out in the actual game itself.

This would give players the most realistic experience for what the change would be like.

The danger is that if we trial risk PvP this way, players could lose items to temporary content that they don't fully understand.

Another caveat is that there are some things that may not be feasible to test this way. However, I think live-beta-testing may be more compatible for PvP than for other things.

I recommend this method because this is how I used to develop my own multiplayer PvP Team Fortress 2 servers, and it was an extremely effective way of working with a community.

It would be very important for this kind of live-beta-testing to **promise** players that it's only a temporary thing with a set time-limit and at the end of the test, it will then be subject to a poll.

I recommend a time-limit of 1-month. I'm just guessing, but I think that would be long enough for initial hype to die off and for players to get a sense of how it plays out over a longer period of time.

After the testing period, the idea then goes to a Lock-In Poll or [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]].
