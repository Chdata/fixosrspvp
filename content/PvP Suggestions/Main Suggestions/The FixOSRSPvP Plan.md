## The FixOSRSPvP Plan

### **Part 1:** Get everyone's attention. Jagex and the community.

We want to create an open dialogue between Jagex and the community.

Joining and sharing the [[FixOSRSPvP]] movement is how you can help us make some noise until people find us.

![[FixOSRSPvP]]

### **Part 2:** Our Big Questions for Jagex

1. We would like to ask Jagex if they are willing to try working with us to figure out a new design process for PvP in OSRS and if Jagex is willing to review or help us develop new PvP suggestions.

2. We would like to ask Jagex for some PvP playercount trackers.

**It would include the following:** (on the official Oldschool Runescape website)
- [ ] An overall OSRS playercount-over-time line graph.
- [ ] An overall PvP playercount tracker including all forms of PvP together.
- [ ] Granularity to check PvP playercounts case-by-case for different PvP activities and minigames.
- [ ] Activity trackers and/or heatmaps for different Wilderness hotspots.

**Reasoning:**
We want this so we can have a public, objective measurement of how well PvP is doing.

### **Part 3:** Working with Jagex and the community

We have a TON of PvP suggestions.

It's too much for anyone to read all at once.

I want to talk with the Jagex team to see if we can work out a way to process these various ideas and figure out a plan of action to better communicate these ideas and hopefully greenlight some real PvP updates.

My recommendation is some kind of process where we go through ideas one-by-one and figure out which idea is better than all of the others.

Then, we would like to know if it would be possible for Jagex to do something like a more iterative process with less polling and more of "let's just try [[Live-Beta-Testing]] some stuff and see how we feel about it?"

But, this is a conversation where we really need to have Jagex on board with us to see what the team thinks.

So, help us **get everyone's attention** and share the [[FixOSRSPvP]] movement today!
