###### TL;DR
The goal of [[FixOSRSPvP]] is to petition Jagex to take action in developing good long-term solutions to create a healthy system of PvP for OSRS, using [[The FixOSRSPvP Plan]].

## The FixOSRSPvP Plan

### **Part 1:** Get everyone's attention. Jagex and the community.

We want to create an open dialogue between Jagex and the community.

Joining and sharing [[FixOSRSPvP]] is how you can help us make some noise until people find us.

Sign the [[FixOSRSPvP]] petition to show Jagex that PvP is WORTH INVESTING IN.

### **Part 2:** Our Big Questions for Jagex

1. We would like to ask Jagex if they are willing to try [[The FixOSRSPvP Plan]].

2. We need data!

We would like to ask Jagex to implement playercount trackers for the PvP population of OSRS.

**It would include the following:**
- [ ] An overall OSRS playercount-over-time line graph.
- [ ] An overall PvP playercount tracker including all forms of PvP together.
- [ ] Granularity to check PvP playercounts case-by-case for different PvP activities and minigames.
- [ ] Activity trackers and/or heatmaps for different Wilderness hotspots.

There is already a [heatmap plugin](https://osrsworldheatmap.com/?h=DEATHS&a=all&x=3153&y=3752&zoom=6) which includes the Wilderness, but it does not include playercount numbers over time.

**Reasoning:**
We want this so we can have a public, objective measurement of how well PvP is doing.

### **Part 3:** Agree on and execute a PvP suggestion design process between Jagex and the community.

### The FixOSRSPvP Design Process Loop

The process we are hoping for is a 3 phase process.

Phase 1: Players (or Jagex) submit PvP suggestions for discussion and approval for phase 2.
Phase 2: The best PvP suggestions are chosen to be developed for Live-Beta-Testing.
Phase 3: The Live-Beta-Tested ideas go through [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]] to choose official updates.

#### Phase 1: PvP Suggestion Phase

##### Step 1: PvP Suggestion Submissions

We are accepting submissions for PvP suggestions in the [[FixOSRSPvP]] Discord.

We have an official "PvP Suggestion Template" that must be filled out.

Anyone may submit their suggestion, and there is no time limit, so you can take your time refining the idea as much as you want before you submit it.

The submissions are given to Jagex for an initial review.

At the time of submission, Jagex may veto the idea if they provide a reason, otherwise Jagex approves it for the next step.

If you're a developer at Jagex, we would love for you to submit your own PvP updates through this process too.

##### Step 2: PvP Suggestion Finalization Process

We wish to discuss various possible solution ideas, one-at-a-time, between Jagex and the community.

[[FixOSRSPvP]] has a collection of many, many proposed solutions.

Step 2 of the design process is about improving those ideas to their best possible form, with just a few discussion questions.

The purpose of this discussion process is to finalize the idea into something players would enjoy which Jagex can realistically develop.

###### PvP Suggestion Discussion Phase Questions

Every suggestion will be given the following questions:

**Opinion Poll:** (Using an official poll via the polling system)

Do you like this idea? (link to idea blogpost here)
1. Yes
2. No
3. Needs Improvement 
4. Skip Vote

If you voted 3, what problems does the idea have?

If you voted 3, what's your proposed solution to improve it?

**Discussion question for everyone:** (in the [[FixOSRSPvP]] Discord)

If we are trying to make this idea work and make PvP work, how can this idea be improved and what is the best possible "form" of the idea?

Jagex and the author of the idea would then take this feedback to further finalize the idea.

**A question for Jagex:**

Is it possible and feasible for Jagex to develop this idea, given the changes?

###### PvP Suggestion Discussion Phase Duration
Each discussion could be given 1 week, or 1 month, or 6 months.

I'm not sure what the best time frame is.

There are so many ideas, I would go for 2 weeks.

After the time is up, if the idea is finalized, it goes to the next step. If it's not finished, it must be re-submitted.

Alternatively, it could be given "as long as it needs" for the idea to be "finalized".

###### PvP Suggestion Finalization

Once the idea is "finalized" to a point where it's pretty much ready for Jagex to develop as-is, move on to the next step.

##### Step 3: PvP Suggestion Ranking

At this point, nothing is locked-in yet for actual development by Jagex nor for permanent addition to the game.

Step 3 is about taking our now-finalized ideas, and ranking them to see what the community is most interested in seeing be developed, utilizing [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]].

The top ranked suggestion graduates to a potential PvP Update.

#### Phase 2: Live-Beta-Testing Phase

##### Step 1: Live-Beta-Testing
Jagex develops the top idea, players playtest it.
##### Step 2: Live-Beta-Testing Feedback, Review, & Revisioning
We refine it further, gather data, review the results (player activity), review player satisfaction with the idea via opinion polling.

The goal here is to end up with a "popularity rating" for how popular the idea is with different factions of the community.
- PvPers
- Potential PvPers
- Non-PvPers
- [[Niche Player Types]]
##### Step 3: "Early-Adoption" Poll

At this point, we can give the PvP suggestion a lock-in poll for permanent addition to the game.

However, there is one difference to our usual polls.

Instead of being a "lock-in poll", this is an "early-adoption poll".

Passing the poll at this point means the PvP suggestion gets to skip the rest of the process and added immediately.

If the idea fails the poll here, it's not considered a permanent failure, instead, the idea must now win a secondary ranking test against other PvP Updates, and is put on the backburner until there are other PvP Update ideas for us to host more [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]].

**Reasoning:** It may be possible for community sentiment to change quickly when the idea is compared to various other ideas.

#### Phase 3: [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]]

Repeat phases 1 & 2 until we've tried many things.

Once we have around 5 potential PvP Updates that have gone through live-beta-testing, we commit them to [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]].

The top ranked PvP Update is then officially released as a permanent addition to the game!

There is an option in the [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]] that says "none of these ideas are good", so it is still possible for every idea to be vetoed by the community at this point.

The runner-ups remain as candidates for future [[PvP Update Ranking Polls]], even if they didn't win this time, and even if "none of these ideas are good" won.

Repeat phases 1, 2, & 3 until "PvP is good now".
