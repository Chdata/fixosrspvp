I think there are two major possible routes for revitalizing PvP.  

**Route 1:** Fix the Wilderness, and then later, safe PvP minigames could get a touch-up too.  
**Route 2:** Ignore the Wilderness (for now) and make safe PvP minigames really good.  

I used to believe in focusing on improving safe PvP minigames because I used to believe the Wilderness was inherently unfixable, and I care about safe PvP minigames more. I think they are more fun.  
  
However, when players talk about PvP, the majority of discussions are about the Wilderness. Most of the gripes about PvP are about the Wilderness. Most of what PKers actually want is to see the Wilderness do well again.  
  
So I've come to realize, the fastest route to seeing safe PvP minigames improved, is probably actually to see the Wilderness get improved first, and then afterwards other forms of PvP can be focused on later maybe after PvP has grown in popularity.  
  
After all, the Wilderness is kind of just like a big open-world minigame. It has its own enclosed system with specific rules that only apply within its area.  
  
I know what I like about minigame PvP and what I dislike about Wilderness PKing. I've studied the community's discussions about the topic for literal years and I know the wishes and pain points of both sides. I come from a background of over a decade of experience in balancing multiplayer PvP for contrasting "groups" of a playerbase.  
  
So I came up with designs that bridge the gap and make reasonable compromises between PvPers and PvMers that don't favor one side or the other too much.  

That is what led me to come up with the major Wilderness solution ideas I'm sharing in this project.
