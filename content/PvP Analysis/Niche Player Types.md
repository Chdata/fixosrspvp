Most people think of the Wilderness in terms of PKers and PvMers, or predator vs prey, or cat vs mouse.

I have some terminology that I commonly use to further differentiate types of players.

# Terminology I use

**PKers** - Players who mostly have a focus on Risk PKing, mainly including the Wilderness, PvP Worlds, and Bounty Hunter.

**PvPers** - Players who enjoy various forms of PvP, including safe PvP minigames, not just Risk PKing.

**Note:** I know that PKers do not like or agree with this distinction, and I am aware that the typical PvMer uses this distinction as a way to mock and ridicule PKers.

I don't agree with those PvMers. I just use this as a way to differentiate Risk PKing from PvP.

Even the PKers I talk to agree that they don't include safe PvP as "true PvP". But I do.

Players used to spend countless hours goofing around in Soul Wars, Castle Wars, Fist of Guthix, and Stealing Creation.

I don't consider myself a PKer because I'm a risk-averse player. I prefer safe PvP minigames. So I'm a PvPer. But I don't necessarily think risk PKing is inherently bad either. It's just poorly designed in its current form and I could see myself enjoying PKing if it were better.

It's all just opinions to me and I consider "PvP" the ambiguous blanket-term for everything, and "PKing" as a subset of PvP that involves risk and usually involves the Wilderness.

**PvMers** - Players who enjoy PvM. When we're talking about the Wilderness, we usually bucket Skillers into this category too.

**Skillers** - Players who enjoy skilling.

**Anti-PKers** - Players who are PvEing in the Wilderness with the express purpose of fighting back against PKers with the benefit of being Unskulled with +4.

**Loot Pinatas** or **Prey Animals** - Players who either just let themselves get killed or do what they can to tank to safety and escape, but are not really in the Wilderness because they enjoy the gameplay, and they don't want to fight back at all ever.

**Indiana Jones** - Wilderness players who enjoy both "risk vs reward" and "escape gameplay" and trend towards being pacifists in a PvP engagement but may fight back sometimes if it's opportune for them to do so or if it helps them escape. They may also enjoy [[the stay in the Wilderness as long as possible without leaving it challenge]].

**PvPvMers** - Players who enjoy PvMing in the Wilderness and fighting whatever challengers come across them. This differs from an anti-PKer because they are just as happy whether they see challengers or not.

**All-Rounders** - Players who enjoy everything or aspire to try everything.

**Fence-Sitters** or **Potential PvPers** - Players who might be interested in PvP, but do not do it because all forms of PvP are poorly designed in their opinion.
