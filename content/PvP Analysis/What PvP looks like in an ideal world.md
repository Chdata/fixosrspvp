This is what I believe an "ideal world" looks like based on [[This is what players really want]] and [[This Is What Players Have Been Saying For The Past 10 Years]].

1. All players engaging with PvP are there because they want to enjoy the PvP gameplay, not because they begrudgingly interact with it in order to obtain an item that they want to use outside of PvP.

In other words, players who are not interested in PvP would no longer be incentivized to engage with PvP.

That alone would resolve a lot of the resentment the community (on the PvM side) has towards PvP.

With less resentment, there would be less hate and spite voting against PvP.

2. All PvP, despite not having unique items that are useful outside of PvP, would still have good enough reward incentive to drive long-term activity to all of our PvP activities.

Whether this means rewards that break down to a certain gold/h value, or cosmetic rewards, or untradeable rewards, or rewards that only function in PvP, or something else, is a question for the future.

3. Casual players would have low skill ways to engage with various forms of PvP, which allows them to enjoy it even if they can't quite reach higher skill levels.

4. The Wilderness would be active on every world and in every hotspot, without having to limit access to the Wilderness to only a certain number of worlds.

5. There would be minimal friction for PvP updates to be released to the game.

These are the goals of [[The FixOSRSPvP Plan]].
