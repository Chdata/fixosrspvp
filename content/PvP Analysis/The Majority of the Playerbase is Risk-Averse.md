I'm a risk-averse PvP enjoyer, meaning I fundamentally dislike PvP in the Wilderness, and I prefer safe PvP minigames.

The community has definitely been vocal for the past 10 years about how very few players actually like to risk or want to risk their items.

1. Many players consider is only natural that most of us are risk-averse, considering how safe and inexpensive PvM deaths are now thanks to Gravestones, Death's Office, and untradeable items being kept on death.

2. We also consider it only natural, because Oldschool Runescape is a game about constant progression and accumulation of wealth and XP. No one likes negative progression, and the Wilderness is the only place where you can lose your bank.

3. It's only natural because the OSRS playerbase has a lot of working adults with limited free time. Losing progression means having wasted time that we spent hours into.

4. And this is only exacerbated by how toxic PKers are in the Wilderness because quite frankly, nobody wants to donate free gold to toxic asshole PKers, giving the playerbase even more reason to not want to risk anything of value.

5. Not to mention, every tutorial and wiki page ever about the Wilderness is always repeating the same message: **Don't bring anything you're not willing to risk losing.**

# What are players willing to lose?

Well, the grand majority of players are not willing to risk losing any of their hard-earned equipment they probably spent hours grinding for.

Many players are not even willing to risk losing 50k knowing that it goes to players who are likely just very toxic.

Risk-averse players like me are not willing to lose 50k because we calculate loss-over-time. 50k in one death isn't much, but as I am not that great at PvP, I expect to be dying over and over again for a long time before I "git gud".

In the Wilderness, you are expected to die. And if you're newer to PvP, you're expected to die even more often.

So, 50k in deaths is just going to constantly add up. It's just not worth it when I can just go to Soul Wars and enjoy PvP for free without having to buy a bunch of return sets.

This "startup cost" of learning means that [[The Barriers to Entry are Too High]] and [[Risk is Expensive]].

Also, I'm not really willing to PvP with a 50k setup (Salad Robes) because using weaker equipment means I'm going to die more.

The Wilderness creates a bad choice triple-bind where there isn't really a viable option for gameplay.

1. Either you bring absolutely nothing and have 0 risk, but cannot possibly fight back, or
2. You bring a minimal risk 50k loadout, and now you have extremely weak equipment so you are very likely to die most of the time anyway, or
3. You bring an actually decent loadout, and now you're risking 300k or 40m depending on what that loadout is, and you are losing a lot more gold on death.

All 3 options suck for new players, casual players, and risk-averse players. The majority of the playerbase.

It's not a problem for PKers because they are both willing to lose huge amounts, [[PKers are desensitized to risk]], and they already have several years of skill and experience built up that they can reliably kill other players to earn back their losses.

None of that is true for newer or casual players.

[[Risk is Not Worth the Reward]]

[[Blessed Status]] and safe PvP minigames resolve all of these problems, in my eyes.
