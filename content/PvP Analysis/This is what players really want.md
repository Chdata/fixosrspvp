
# Here is each faction's most simplified desires:

1. From what I understand, the PvP side just wants it to be active. Mainly the Wilderness.

2. The PvM side just wants to not feel forced to engage with it.

3. Ironmen just want gameplay that stays true to the integrity of Ironman mode.

4. Jagex wants the entire playerbase to be happy.

# There are a few more specific desires from each faction.

**PKer's most common requests:**
1. PKers want to [[Limit the Wilderness to only be accessible on X number of worlds.]] Let's say 100 instead of 300.
   - Some have said only 10 worlds and any number in between. 50. 75.
   - The underlying desire is to reduce hopscape and be able to find other players easier.
   - In other words, the PvP side just wants the Wilderness to be active.

2. PKers want the return of multi revs.
   - In other words, the PvP side just wants the Wilderness to be active.

3. PKers want the removal of the PJ timer or the return of "singles teams".
   
4. PKers generally want to keep the Wilderness a "high risk vs high reward zone", and an "open PvP zone where anything goes" and they generally want to keep the food chain and predator prey design.
  - In other words, they want to retain the "traditional" or "nostalgic" design.

**PvMer's most common requests:**
1. PvMers want the content that makes them feel "forced" to engage with PvP, to be available outside of PvP.

2. PvMers want to [[Make PvP opt-in in the Wilderness]] like RS3 did. *(Please no, that is a horrible idea for OSRS.)*

3. PvMers don't want to get "interrupted" or attacked at all while doing PvM or skilling content in the Wilderness.
   
4. PvMers want less predator prey or "loot pinata" content, or its complete removal or optionality.

**I've heard this from Ironmen a handful of times:**
1. Ironmen don't want overpowered early game rewards as what the Wilderness offers compared to what the baseline of their gameplay is like without the Wilderness.

2. Ironmen generally want the same things as PvMers.

  - Source 1: Sae Bae:  [Marstead - Why OSRS Succeeds, Pitfalls of RS3, Dry Protection, Wildy, Trade | Sae Bae Cast 295](https://youtu.be/Qx9j2rA3kcU&t=6596)
  - Timestamp: 1:49:56
  - Source 2: Danil445's [Reimagining the Wilderness Experience: A Holistic Approach to the Future of the Wilderness]([https://www.reddit.com/gallery/wnnk66](https://www.reddit.com/r/2007scape/comments/wnnk66/reimagining_the_wilderness_experience_a_holistic/))
  - He includes his own proposed solution for this regarding the Revenant Caves.

**Jagex's stated goals, development process, and design rules:**
1. Jagex wants the entire playerbase to be happy with PvP. Both sides.

2. Jagex does not want to inflate gold in the economy.

3. Jagex does not want to "cause riots" from any faction of the community.

4. Jagex wants to stick to the polling system and the "community-driven" nature of the game.

I don't have specific sources for this, but if you watch various OSRS Official Podcasts and discussions with JMods and read blogposts, these statements from various Jagex Developers commonly come up.

# Summary

These conflicting goals create [[PvP Game Design Double-Binds]] which are very difficult, or impossible, to reconcile with each other without giving up on something or someone's opinions.
