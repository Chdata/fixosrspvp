This refers to gameplay where one player is hunting another player and the other player is usually just trying to escape, often while carrying some kind of reward out of the gameplay arena.

The [[Identity of the Wilderness]] is largely regarded to be this style of gameplay, and it gives rise to [[Loot Pinata Content]].

Types of players who enjoy this include some [[Niche Player Types]] such as [[Indiana Jones]] and [[PvPvMers]].
