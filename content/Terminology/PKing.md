PKing is short for Player-Killing.

PKers are one of the [[Niche Player Types]].

Unfortunately, [[The community is divided.]]

Players are starting to form two different definitions of what they believe PKing is.

**PKers:** PKing is a form of PvP. They mean the same thing.

**PvMers:** PKing is the act of killing another player regardless of circumstances, including griefing [[Loot Pinatas]] who don't want to fight back. This is different from PvP, which is two players fighting to the death.

As far as I can tell, this definition is used to mock PKers.

**And my definition:**
PKing is a shorthand for "Risk PvP", including the Wilderness and Venge PKing in PvP Worlds and Bounty Hunter.

PKing is an ambiguous term. There is no de facto definition anymore.

There is a similar debate about what the definition of [[True PvP]] is.
