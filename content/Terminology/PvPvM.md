PvPvM refers to engaging in PvM content with the risk of getting attacked by players.

**[[PvPvMers]]** are one of the [[Niche Player Types]], who are players who enjoy PvMing in the Wilderness and fighting whatever challengers come across them. This differs from an anti-PKer because they are just as happy whether they see challengers or not.

It is somewhat implied that skilling is included in this, so it's technically PvPvE and PvPvEers, but OSRS players don't really use the term "PvE" much.
