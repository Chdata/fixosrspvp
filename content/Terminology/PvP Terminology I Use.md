# Forinthry

Many of my PvP suggestions use the term "Forinthry" a certain way.

Forinthry was the past name of the [Wilderness](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wilderness) when it was part of the [Zarosian Empire](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zarosian_Empire).

For me, it has a tone of a "forgotten" and "lost civilization" vibe to it.

[[Forinthry Gates & Forinthry Artefacts]]
[[Forinthry Castle]]
[[Forinthry Caskets]]

In terms of game design, I'm using the term in a similar way to how **blighted** symbolizes items that are only able to be used in PvP.

**Forinthry:** Things that give benefits in the Wilderness, such as reducing risk or saving things on death, or buffs in areas of the Wilderness.

It's phonetically similar to Fortitude or Fortune. It's a reference to the once existing rich  [Zarosian Empire](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zarosian_Empire), as if we are resurfacing scarce, lost powers to our benefit.
