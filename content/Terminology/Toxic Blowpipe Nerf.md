Many OSRS players already know this one, but the Toxic Blowpipe Nerf is referring to the time ... the Toxic Blowpipe... was Nerfed.

This was done unpolled as an integrity change, with lots and lots of statistics and research.

The official announcement for this going live to the game was in the [Equipment Rebalancing Updated](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/equipment-rebalancing-updated?oldschool=1) blogpost on February 8th, 2021.

Jagex refers to it as [2021's infamous Toxic Blowpipe nerf](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/project-rebalance---item--combat-adjustments?oldschool=1).

The reason it was nerfed was because:
1. It was too insanely good everywhere in the game.
2. It ruined design space for new ranged weapons to be added to the game because it was too good everywhere. Jagex was setting up for the longevity of the game.

# Player sentiment at the time

When all of this was fresh, a lot of players were very against this nerf.

Part of the controversy around it was because it lasted 6 years in the state it was before it got nerfed.

# Postponement to Release

[Jagex even decided to postpone it for a few months before actually pushing it into the game.](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/equipment-rebalance-postponed?oldschool=1)

Ultimately, it did get into the game later though.

[The proposal was brought up again on June 14th.](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/equipment-rebalance-ranged-meta-proposal?oldschool=1)

[And then the nerf finally made it into the game on June 30th.](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/phosanis-nightmare?oldschool=1)

# Aftermath

Players got used to it.

The community moved on and we generally understand it had to happen for good reasons.

Jagex stands by the decision and some of the developers have mentioned years later that they are glad it was done because it did make future design space easier to navigate.

Anyway, it was a huge event for the Oldschool Runescape playerbase.

It rebalanced a weapon that was widely used for 6 years, and the process of changing it took more than half a year of focus on this one item.

It will forever serve as an example that players will always be against [[Negative-Sum Balance Changes]].
