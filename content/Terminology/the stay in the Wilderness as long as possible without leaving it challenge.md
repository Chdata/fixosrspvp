A lot of designs in the Wilderness naturally point to this [[the stay in the Wilderness as long as possible without leaving it challenge]].

**For example:**
- The Looting Bag allows you to accumulate PvE drops without taking up your inventory space.
- Wilderness Bosses, Revenants, and the Wilderness Agility Course all drop blighted food and potion to keep you topped up.
- Wilderness Bosses and the Wilderness Agility Course all drop noted items so you don't have to bank.
- You can use the [Amulet of Avarice](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Amulet_of_avarice) to get noted drops from Revenants so you don't have to bank.
- The Wilderness somewhat leans into some forms of escape gameplay, such as the [Escape Caves](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Escape_Caves).
- Players currently use the [[Freeze-Log Meta]] to engage with this challenge.
