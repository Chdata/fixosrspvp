There are a couple of things I call "[[Sacred Designs]]" of the Wilderness.

These are things that PKers would agree are inherent and integral to what the Wilderness is.

- It's a open world PvP area.
- It's a high risk, high reward area.
- The deeper you go, the riskier it gets.

I am not looking to change that much with [[FixOSRSPvP]], but I do want to simplify one point:

- It's a risk vs reward area.

My overall design goal is to stick as close as possible to the [[Sacred Designs]] that make up the [[Identity of the Wilderness]].

My reason for changing that slightly is that [[The Majority of the Playerbase is Risk-Averse]] and [[Risk is Not Worth the Reward]].

[[Blessed Status]] aims to provide a **medium risk, medium reward** and a **scaled risk, scaled reward** form of gameplay to the Wilderness because I believe that is what is missing from the Wilderness. Something that new players, casual players, and risk-averse players can enjoy.

[[Forinthry Gates & Forinthry Artefacts]] and [[Cursed Wilderness Equipment]] have similar goals of offering alternatives to risk.

High risk is an incredibly small niche of players. We NEED less risky options if we are to see the population grow.

# Jagex's Definition of the Wilderness

["Relive the challenging levelling system and **risk-it-all PvP** of the biggest retro styled MMO."](https://osrs.game/)

The release of the Wilderness in 2001: [Wilderness system online](https://runescape.wiki/w/Update:Wilderness_system_online)

The new wilderness area of runescape is online! This is a huge new area which is part of an improved player-combat system which was first proposed a few months ago. A poll on the website at the time showed the vast majority of players in favour of the new system, so we have now written it.

In the improved system player fighting is only allowed in the new wilderness area. The ability to switch between PK and Non-PK will be removed and instead you just go into the dangerous wilderness area if you want to fight other players, or stay within the current map area if you don't.

**The further you go into the wilderness the more dangerous it becomes, but the more treasure you could find! How much of a risk you want to take is entirely up to you.**
