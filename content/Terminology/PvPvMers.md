Players who enjoy [[PvPvM]], or PvMing in the Wilderness and fighting whatever challengers come across them. This differs from an anti-PKer because they are just as happy whether they see challengers or not.

[[PvPvMers]] have a lot of crossover with [[Indiana Jones]] but may not necessarily be interested in escape gameplay.

[[PvPvMers]] have a lot of crossover with [[Anti-PKers]] but don't necessarily mind not being attacked either.
