[[The community is divided.]] and cannot agree on what [[True PvP]] is.

**PKers:** Risk [[PKing]]: The Wilderness, PvP Worlds, Bounty Hunter. NOT safe PvP minigames.

Risk provides a thrill and stakes that safe PvP does not.

**Everyone else:** Two players fighting to the death. Or two equal teams fighting to the death. NOT [[PKing]] the [[Loot Pinatas]].

**Myself:** All PvP is [[True PvP]].

Some forms of it are better than others.

I lean towards the "everyone else" definition and I feel that risk gets in the way of [[True PvP]].

But [[Predator vs Prey]] is still [[True PvP]] as much as anything else.

There's still one player (a hunter) versus another player (the one trying to escape or outplay the hunter).

It's just not developed to its full potential of having [[Real Wilderness Escape Gameplay]] where PKers are only [[PKing]] the [[Indiana Jones]] players and [[PvPvMers]].

And imo, it's dumb that [[Loot Pinatas]] are incentivized to engage at all.
