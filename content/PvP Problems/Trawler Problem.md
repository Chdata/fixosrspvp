PvP in Oldschool Runescape is what I like to call a "Trawler Problem".

What I mean is that PvP is literally like the [Fishing Trawler](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fishing_Trawler) minigame.

PvP is a boat, there's a lot of holes in the side, and this ship is SINKING.

Fixing just one or two problems while leaving the others means the ship is still sinking.

For example even if we fix [[The Skill Gap is Too Large]], [[Risk is Not Worth the Reward]] is still a problem.

Even if we fix [[The Barriers to Entry are Too High]], [[The Polling System is Incompatible with PvP]] is still a problem.

Even if we fix all of that, that doesn't guarantee that we've fixed [[Predator-Prey Design Problems]].

This also explains why PKers feel like PvMers are "moving goalposts" every time one pain point of the Wilderness gets solved, and yet we still have another to complain about.

1. We got the Skull Prevention option and still hate the Wilderness.
2. We got the PJ timer change and still hate the Wilderness.
3. We got the Dragon Pickaxe made available outside of the Wilderness and still hate the Wilderness.
4. Next up are the Voidwaker, Ma2 capes, and Wilderness Clue Scroll Steps, but we're still going to hate the Wilderness because [[We aren't addressing the core problems.]]

This feeling of "moving goalposts" happens because there are just that many problems with PvP.

So of course fixing just 1 or 2 holes doesn't fix the entire ship.

# Solving the Trawler Problem

There are 2 ways out!

1. You just have to combo enough solutions together at once that you patch every hole in the wall!
2. Or you just have to patch each hole one-by-one until it is done.

If you actually fix everything, then our ship will stop sinking.

Yes, it will take years of work to undo years of neglect.

That is the goal of [[FixOSRSPvP]].
