Literally [like this](https://youtu.be/gYH0D52fXe8)

There are two major factions of debates about PvP.

1. PvMers (roughly 90% of the voting population)
2. PKers (roughly 10% of the voting population)

See: [[OSRS Population Stats]]

The problem with any of them trying to have a dialogue together about anything is that they are coming it from completely different and separate perspectives.

They can be talking about the same thing (the Wilderness) yet be describing two completely separate realities.

A PvMer's gameplay and meta is a completely different game from a PKer's gameplay and meta.

The discussions only create [cognitive dissonance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_dissonance), triggering the [backfire effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belief_perseverance) and getting both sides to further reinforce their own biases.

Almost no one really tries to meet in the middle.

The different factions have a huge conflict of interests in the Wilderness.

Incidentally, there isn't a huge conflict of interest in any other form of PvP, because no other form of PvP incentivizes you to engage with it for rewards that are useful outside of PvP.

There are some other unique "factions" of these discussions.

1. Mains who don't PvP (roughly 60% of the voting population)
2. Ironmen (roughly 30% of the voting population)
3. Jagex Developers
4. "Traditional" PKers who prefer older, nostalgic designs.
5. "Modern" PvPers who prefer newer designs and reworks.

See: [[OSRS Population Stats]]

And they are all having completely different conversations that don't cross together well.

The cherry on top of this problem is that OSRS's polling system requires a 70% supermajority to pass any updates, yet none of these factions are fundamentally able to agree with each other on any ideas whatsoever.

Also that 30% Ironmen population is completely de-incentivized from interacting with PvP, so that's almost a guaranteed 30% no vote.

Essentially we have RED vs BLU except we're trying to get them to agree on polls together. They can't.

Therefore, [[The Polling System is Incompatible with PvP]]
