Both Risk and Reward are terrible for Ironmen.

[[Risk is Not Worth the Reward]] is a big problem for Mains, but even worse for Ironmen.

1. Reward: Ironmen earn nothing from killing other players in the Wilderness, making reward incentive non-existent to PK or to even fight back.

2. Risk: Ironmen take significantly longer to obtain any gear in the game, making their risk from losing items astronomically different from mains in terms of time value for equipment they use.

So, I have a series of suggestions to [[Make Wilderness PvP viable for Ironmen]].
