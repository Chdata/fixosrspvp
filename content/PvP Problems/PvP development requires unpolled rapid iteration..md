You will never find another successful multiplayer PvP game that has a polling system quite like Oldschool Runescape's, because [[The Polling System is Incompatible with PvP]].

Multiplayer PvP gameplay by nature can have tons of unpredictable ripple effects even from small changes.

It does not serve us if we make a PvP change, and then it creates new problems, and those new problems are then not addressed for the next 5 years or next 10 years.

As was the case with all of these changes:
- Wilderness Rejuvenation
- Multi Revenants
- PJ Timer
- Castle Wars and Soul Wars rewards buff and then nerf
- Last Man Standing rewards
- PvP Arena design, rewards, and match forfeiting
- Cheating, fairness, and integrity problems in PvP

Everything, all of it had all sorts of proposals about how to do better or what the "next steps" could look like.

But Jagex has a pattern where, if it doesn't get fixed within 1-2 weeks of trying 1 fix, then the project gets completely abandoned for 5 or more years.

And then, because it's left in a bad spot, resentment towards all of these activities slowly increases in the background to the point that players no longer believe it's possible or worthwhile to even try any potential fixes for them.
