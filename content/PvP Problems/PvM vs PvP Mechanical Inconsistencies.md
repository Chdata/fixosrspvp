


-  PvMers are used to a low-risk gravestone system in every part of the game which contrasts high-risk PvP starkly, which creates a huge perception gap of risk between PvMers and PvPers.
-  The elemental tomes (fire, water, earth) have wildly different stats in PvM and PvP
-  Autocasting on staves behaves differently between PvP and PvM contexts, creating confusion for players transitioning between modes.
-  Autocast settings are not just inconsistent between PvM and PvP. They're inconsistent between PvP and PvP. Autocast works different in the Wilderness, in Castle Wars, in Soul Wars, in the PvP Arena, in Bounty Hunter, and so on and so forth. It's a core game mechanic and even some of the developers at Jagex have acknowledged they'd rather clear this one up. You can find quotes in the official OSRS PvP Feedback Discord.
-  Most powered staves are disabled in PvP, which is counterintuitive and limits the viability of several otherwise useful magic weapons when they could just have a PvP-specific nerf similar to the Toxic Blowpipe.
-  The Toxic Blowpipe is inconsistent with the Camphor, Ironwood, and Rosewood Blowpipes with the Toxic Blowpipe being the only Blowpipe that fires 1 tick slower in PvP.
-  Spellbook and prayer icon resizing is disabled in PvP - a small but representative example of many differences that accumulate into a confusing and annoying experience. Players cannot comfortably use the interface setups they are used to, and are instead forced to play with a generalized setup should they choose to engage with the content at all.
-  Trouver Parchment and broken equipment mechanics in the Wilderness are confusing
-  There are hidden mechanics that have a huge affect on PvP that are never explained in-game. PID, triple eating, hitsplat stacking, spec comboing, etc.
