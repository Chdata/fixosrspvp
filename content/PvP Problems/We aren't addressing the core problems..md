Jagex's solution to "revitalizing the Wilderness" has always been to just add new monsters, bosses, and skilling content with rewards into the Wilderness.

These are all the same thing: [[Loot Pinata Content]].

The majority opinion on the PvM side is that Loot Pinata content is failing and sucks.
The majority opinion on the PvP side is that it's fundamentally necessary for the Wilderness.
My opinion is that both are correct.

None of the [[Loot Pinata Content]] addresses [[The real, core problems of the Wilderness]]:
[[The Barriers to Entry are Too High]].
[[The Skill Gap is Too Large]]
[[Risk is Not Worth the Reward]]
[[The Majority of the Playerbase is Risk-Averse]].
[[The Wilderness Worldhopping Problem]]
[[The Logout Meta of the Wilderness Sucks]]

Some of these do try to do some things about [[Predator-Prey Design Problems]].

For example, the Wilderness Agility Course Buff fills your inventory with blighted food and potions while also requiring a Looting Bag so your inventory can be free to hold PKing supplies.

Wilderness bosses and Revenants also drop blighted food and potions.

The Wilderness Bosses also had the [Escape Caves](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Escape_Caves) built in as part of them to try and create some [[Real Wilderness Escape Gameplay]].

# Core problems I disagree with

There is a rising sentiment on the PvM side that they fundamentally just don't want to get attacked at all while doing PvM or Skilling, and they don't want to fight back at all, so they are asking to [[Make PvP opt-in in the Wilderness]].

The [[Identity of the Wilderness]] is that it's a PvP area and I am not looking to change that with [[FixOSRSPvP]].

To [[Make PvP opt-in in the Wilderness]] would just be a big "fuck you" to all the PKers.

We are trying to find middlegrounds, not kill off a section of the playerbase.

The solution for these PvMers is that they should not be incentivized to go there, by [[Making the Wilderness Truly Optional]].

The PvP area of the game should only be a place for players who are interested in PvP and [[PvPvM]].
